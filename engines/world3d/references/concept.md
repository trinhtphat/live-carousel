# Concept — one world, six slides

**The world is the idea.** Not "six product cards": one place the brand lives in, continuous across every seam,
so swiping feels like the camera tracking along it. Examples: a concert stage (ENCORE: the clothes are the band),
the sea from dawn to sunrise (OFFSHORE), a scan lab (PEARL LAB). Others that work: a kitchen counter that runs the
whole length (food), a night street with shop windows (fashion), a desk landscape (SaaS), a forest floor (outdoor),
a giant version of the product itself (skincare bottle as architecture).

**Slide roles (6 by default, 8 s each, the big moment on ~4.0 s = the downbeat of bar 3):**
| # | role | job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook | the world + the promise in ≤ 6 words; something lands hard at 4.0; "swipe →" |
| 2–4 | products | each product does something only it can do in this world (the boot stomps the drum, the board explodes into its layers, the aligner gets scanned) + 2–3 labelled details |
| 5 | finale | everything together, the loudest moment (confetti, flames, the sun flares) |
| 6 | drop / CTA | date, offer, comment keyword; the world closes (curtain, sunset, light down) |

**Seams are persistent:** what crosses an edge (a cable, a waterline, light beams, particles, a board half in the
next slide) must be there at every moment, because people swipe at random times. Plan one bridge per seam.

**Make it feel alive (the user called flat 2016-style motion "mid"):** 3+ things moving at all times; real weight
(squash, overshoot, settle); one impact per slide (camera punch, flash, shockwave); depth (back / mid / front at
different speeds); light that reacts to the beat (`kickEnv`). Bright, saturated worlds read better in the feed than
near-black voids. Type is part of the scene (chrome, poster, marker, flip boards), never a plain fade-in.

**Copy:** a 2–5 word headline per slide, one supporting line, real product facts only. The CTA slide says exactly
what to do ("comment ENCORE", "book your first scan free", "Saturday 6 AM").

Write the plan into `BRIEF.md` (world · palette · per slide: role, what we see, the 4.0 moment, the seam bridge,
copy) and get one "build it" before writing code.
